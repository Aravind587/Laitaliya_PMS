# Interior Company – Task Management System (PMS)

An Angular 18 Enterprise Task & Project Management System designed specifically for Interior Architecture & Contracting Companies, faithfully implementing the dual-perspective workflow (Admin & Site Manager) and the complete Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) across all 7 operational flows.

---

## 🌟 Standardized Turnkey Execution Flows

The system implements the complete operational flows down to trade responsibilities, core objectives, deliverables, action checklists, and exit quality gates:

### 1. 💼 Sales Flow (6 Steps)
`[ Lead Capture & Initial Contact ] ➔ [ Initial Presentation / Demo ] ➔ [ Scope Alignment ] ➔ [ Rough Quotation ] ➔ [ Token Amount Request ] ➔ [ Handover & Kickoff ]`
- **Step 1: Lead Capture & Initial Contact** – Action: Receive lead in CRM, assign to sales rep, contact within 1–4 hrs. Goal: 5–10 min discovery call (budget, timeline, requirements).
- **Step 2: Initial Presentation / Demo** – Action: Present customized capabilities & finishes demo. Goal: Demonstrate clear value & get cost estimate agreement.
- **Step 3: Scope Alignment** – Action: Summarize takeaways, outline deliverables, assumptions & constraints. Goal: Ensure expectations match proposal.
- **Step 4: Rough Quotation** – Action: Issue preliminary non-binding quote with phase payment milestones. Goal: Validate financial fit before technical drafting.
- **Step 5: Token Amount Request** – Action: Request 5%–10% token deposit + Letter of Intent (LOI). Goal: Lock in client commitment & reserve slot.
- **Step 6: Handover & Kickoff** – Action: Issue confirmation receipt, draft SOW, introduce PM & Designer. Goal: Smooth transition to execution.

### 2. 📐 Design Flow (7 Phases)
`[ Site Measurements ] ➔ [ Initial 3D Design ] ➔ [ Material & Finish Selections ] ➔ [ Final 3D Design ] ➔ [ Site Validation ] ➔ [ Final 2D / Production Drawings ] ➔ [ KT to Execution Team ]`
| Phase | Stage Name | Trade / Team | Core Objective | Key Deliverables / Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Site Measurements | Surveyor / Tech Designer | Capture accurate physical dimensions & site conditions | As-built site dimensions, beam/column locations, utility points |
| **02** | Initial 3D Design | 3D Designer / Architect | Concept visualization & spatial layout planning | Conceptual 3D views, spatial flow, tentative color schemes |
| **03** | Selections | Material Specialist | Finalize materials, finishes, fixtures & hardware | Material board sign-off (laminates, veneers, fabrics, hardware) |
| **04** | Final 3D Design | Senior 3D Visualizer | High-precision visualization based on selected materials | Photorealistic final 3D renders with exact materials applied |
| **05** | Site Validation | PM / Site Engineer | Re-verify site conditions against approved design | On-site markup/check for plumbing, electrical & structural fit |
| **06** | Final 2D Drawings | CAD Draftsman | Prepare technical drawings for factory/site manufacturing | Working drawings (elevations, sections, electrical, plumbing) |
| **07** | KT to Execution | Design Lead & PM | Knowledge transfer to project managers & site supervisors | Design handoff meeting, drawing pack sign-off, execution briefing |

### 3. 🏗️ False Ceiling Flow (10 Stages)
`[ Floor Protection ] ➔ [ Framing & Channeling ] ➔ [ Thermal Insulation ] ➔ [ Electrical Wiring ] ➔ [ AC Copper Piping ] ➔ [ Ceiling Woodwork ] ➔ [ Board Fixing ] ➔ [ Painting Sub-tasks ] ➔ [ Final Electrical Fitting ] ➔ [ Site Cleaning ]`
| Stage | Step Name | Primary Trade / Team | Core Objective | Key Deliverables / Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Floor Protection | Site Operations | Safeguard finished floors against scratches & debris | Protective sheet/Gypsum board covering |
| **02** | Framing & Channeling | Ceiling Fabricator | Install metal grid framework for ceiling support | Levelled perimeter & main GI grid structure |
| **03** | Thermal Insulation | Insulation Specialist | Install sound/heat insulation layer in grid cavity | Rockwool / Glasswool insulation laid in place |
| **04** | Electrical Wiring | Electrical Team | Lay ceiling conduits and pull electrical cables | Conduits secured & wire drop-points created |
| **05** | AC Copper Piping | HVAC Technician | Route refrigerant lines and drain pipes for AC units | Pressure-tested AC copper lines & drain outlets |
| **06** | Ceiling Woodwork | Carpenter / Interior Team | Install wooden features, coves, or paneling structures | Wooden framing/coving support ready for board |
| **07** | Board Fixing | Ceiling Installer | Mount gypsum, calcium silicate, or cement boards | Screwed & aligned board ceiling surface |
| **08** | Painting Sub-tasks | Painter / Finishing Team | Achieve smooth, seamless ceiling surface & final color | Joint taping, Putty, Primer, & Final Paint coats |
| **09** | Final Electrical Fitting | Electrical Team | Cut fixture openings & install light fittings | COB lights, strip lights, & fan fittings mounted |
| **10** | Site Cleaning | Housekeeping / Ops | Clean ceiling surface, remove floor protection & debris | Handover-ready site with clean ceiling & floors |

### 4. 🧱 Civil Work Flow (5 Stages)
`[ Demolition ] ➔ [ Reconstruction / Masonry ] ➔ [ Punning / Plastering ] ➔ [ Plumbing Work ] ➔ [ Electrical Work ]`
- **01. Demolition** (Demolition Crew) – Cleared site & rubble/debris disposal.
- **02. Reconstruction** (Masonry Team) – Blockwork/brickwork walls & door/window frames set.
- **03. Punning / Plastering** (Plastering Team) – Level, plumb, and smooth 90-degree wall surfaces.
- **04. Plumbing Work** (Plumbing Team) – Concealed CPVC/UPVC lines & hydro-static pressure tests.
- **05. Electrical Work** (Electrical Team) – Wall chased conduits, flush back-boxes & circuit loops.

---

### 5. 🪚 Manual Work Flow (On-Site Carpentry & Finishing - 5 Stages)
`[ Woodwork / Carpentry ] ➔ [ Stone / Marble Work ] ➔ [ Polish / PU Works ] ➔ [ Wall Painting ] ➔ [ Final Cleaning ]`
- **01. Woodwork (Carpentry)** (Carpenter / Joiner) – Framed cabinets, door frames, paneling, and moldings set.
- **02. Stone & Marble Work** (Stonemason / Tile Layer) – Installed countertops, edge moldings & epoxy grouted joints.
- **03. Polishing / PU Works** (Wood Finisher / Polish Artisan) – Premium veneer & solid wood PU/polished surfaces.
- **04. Wall Painting** (Painting Crew) – Evenly coated, stain-free wall surfaces with clean cut-lines.
- **05. Final Cleaning** (Deep Cleaning Team) – Spotless, move-in/handover-ready interior space.

---

### 6. 🏭 Production Flow (9 Stages)
`[ Drawing Forwarded to Factory ] ➔ [ Factory Cutlist Generation ] ➔ [ R1 (PO) Review - PM ] ➔ [ R2 (PO) Recheck ] ➔ [ Production Approval ] ➔ [ Material List Generation (BOM) ] ➔ [ Material Availability Check ] ➔ [ Material Procurement ] ➔ [ Production Start ]`
| Stage | Step Name | Primary Responsibility | Core Objective | Key Deliverable / Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Drawing Forwarded to Factory | Design / Technical Team | Hand over approved 2D drawings to factory | Approved Production Drawing Package |
| **02** | Factory Cutlist Generation | Factory Technical Team | Convert 2D drawings into precise panel cutlists | Draft Cutlist & Hardware Breakdown |
| **03** | R1 (PO) Review | Project Manager (PM) | First-level technical & quantity verification against site | R1 Reviewed Cutlist with Feedback/Corrections |
| **04** | R2 (PO) Recheck | Senior Tech Lead / PM | Second-level verification to prevent material wastage | Final Checked Cutlist |
| **05** | Production Approval | Management / Ops Head | Sign off on validated cutlists to authorise procurement | Approved Production Order (PO Sign-Off) |
| **06** | Material List Generation | Factory Purchase / Estimator | Generate Bill of Materials (BOM) based on approved cutlist | Bill of Materials (BOM) / Requisition Sheet |
| **07** | Material Availability Check | Warehouse / Store Manager | Audit current stock against required BOM | Inventory Audit Report (In-Stock vs. Shortfall) |
| **08** | Material Procurement | Procurement Team | Purchase required raw materials & specialized hardware | Purchase Orders Issued & Goods Received Note (GRN) |
| **09** | Production Start | Production Supervisor | Issue materials to shop floor and commence manufacturing | Job Card Issued & Cutting/Assembly Underway |

---

### 7. 📦 Modular Flow (7 Stages)
`[ Carcase Assembly ] ➔ [ Drawer Systems ] ➔ [ Lighting Fixing ] ➔ [ Accessories Fitting ] ➔ [ Shutters & Visibles ] ➔ [ Dummies & Skirting ] ➔ [ Ready to Inspect (L1 & L2) ]`
- **01. Carcase Assembly** (Modular Installer) – Square, plumb, and wall-anchored carcass structures.
- **02. Drawers Installation** (Modular Installer) – Smooth-sliding, aligned tandem/drawer boxes.
- **03. Lighting Fixing** (Electrician / Modular Tech) – Concealed LED profile channels, drivers & proximity sensors.
- **04. Accessories Fitting** (Modular Installer) – Fitted cutlery trays, corner units, larders & pull-outs.
- **05. Shutters & Visibles** (Modular Installer) – Aligned shutter fronts with uniform 2mm reveal gaps.
- **06. Dummies & Skirting** (Modular Installer) – Sealed cabinet bases with waterproof PVC skirting & pelmets.
- **07. Ready to Inspect (L1 & L2 QC)** (QC Lead / PM) – Level 1 internal site check + Level 2 audit against production drawings & BOM.

## 👥 Dual-Perspective Roles

1. **👑 Admin Perspective**:
   - Executive dashboard (Total, In Progress, Completed, Overdue)
   - Create projects with Dry/Wet/Utility Kitchen models
   - Manage projects and inspect 9-phase workspaces
   - Daily Tasks & Forgotten Alerts resolution (Ping Site Manager, Mark In Progress, Reschedule)
   - Team management & Login audit log viewer

2. **👷 Site Manager Perspective**:
   - Assigned site overview & Today's tasks
   - SOP Task Execution with status updates & site inspection remarks
   - Overdue & forgotten tasks management
   - Real-time notifications feed & Field Execution SOP

## 🚀 Running the Project Locally

```bash
# 1. Start the Data Server (persists to data/ folder on port 3000)
npm run data-server

# 2. In another terminal, start the Angular App (port 4200)
npm run start -- --port 4200

# 3. Production build
npm run build
```

Open your browser at:
👉 **`http://localhost:4200/`**
